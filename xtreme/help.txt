USB Gate

Makes this Cybiko the Internet gate (CyWIG) for the Cybikos around it, with a PC on the USB cable fetching the pages (cybiko-cywig's PC tool).

Start the PC tool, plug the cable in and leave this running. Other Cybikos then find the gate in their WAP browser and in Browser.

While it runs, this Cybiko is a USB device of its own (0B66 0042) that Windows sets up by itself, so no driver needs installing. It leaves USB for a moment when it starts and stops.

Esc: stop the gate
